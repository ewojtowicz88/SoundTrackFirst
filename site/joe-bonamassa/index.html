1Happier TimesJoe BonamassaNew Hartford, New York — 1977. Joe Bonamassa grows up surrounded by guitars and records. Encouraged by his father, he picks up the guitar at age four.
2Sloe GinJoe BonamassaThe obsession takes hold. Joe immerses himself in blues and British blues-rock, spending countless hours learning the music and language of the guitarists who came before him.
3Steal Your Heart AwayJoe Bonamassa“Smokin’ Joe.” Still a child, Joe begins playing weekend gigs around upstate New York and Pennsylvania. School during the week. Clubs on the weekend.
4Lonesome Road BluesJoe Bonamassa1989 — age 12. Joe opens for B.B. King. The opportunity becomes one of the defining early moments of his life and career.
5Feelin GoodJoe BonamassaThe impossible becomes plausible. B.B. King treats the young guitarist as a musician rather than a novelty. A professional life in music suddenly feels possible.
6Another Kind of LoveJoe BonamassaThe prodigy becomes public. Television, interviews and major blues artists begin drawing attention to the young guitarist from upstate New York.
7Slow TrainJoe BonamassaAdolescence on the road. Joe joins Bloodline, records an album and experiences life inside a working band before eventually choosing his own path.
8One of These DaysJoe BonamassaStart again. No longer simply the child prodigy or a member of someone else’s band, Joe commits to becoming a solo artist.
9You Upset Me BabyJoe Bonamassa2000 — A New Day Yesterday. His first solo album arrives. The career is now his own, but the audience still has to be built.
10Night LifeJoe BonamassaOne room at a time. Joe tours relentlessly, building an audience city by city, club by club and eventually across Europe.
11Reconsider BabyJoe BonamassaRoyal Albert Hall — May 4, 2009. Joe headlines the legendary venue. Eric Clapton joins him onstage. Two decades of work reach a defining breakthrough moment.
