19 / SEQUENCE

5 songs · two stories · unbound narratives

Two stories. One fixed score.

STORY A

  1. 01

    Spotless — Zach Bryan feat. The Lumineers

    Zach Bryan feat. The Lumineers

    He gets home. She’s pulling weeds in the cracked driveway. They’re tired, a little irritated, but still clearly connected.

  2. 02

    O-o-h Child — The Five Stairsteps

    The Five Stairsteps

    She says she wants one good day that doesn’t feel like the same old routine. They decide to go do something together.

  3. 03

    I Say a Little Prayer — Aretha Franklin

    Aretha Franklin

    They both get ready for the day. Slightly awkward, slightly funny, trying harder than usual.

  4. 04

    The Way You Look Tonight — Tony Bennett

    Tony Bennett

    They end up dancing somewhere unexpectedly nice. He sees her differently. She sees that he does.

  5. 05

    If You Wanna Be Happy — Jimmy Soul

    Jimmy Soul

    The night gets goofy. They end up laughing, dancing, and enjoying how imperfect the whole thing is.

The soundtrack does not change. The film does.

STORY B

  1. 01

    Spotless — Zach Bryan feat. The Lumineers

    Zach Bryan feat. The Lumineers

    Same opening. She’s there physically, but mentally somewhere else.

  2. 02

    O-o-h Child — The Five Stairsteps

    The Five Stairsteps

    She lies awake staring at the ceiling. She knows she’s in a dark place, but she also knows she’s not giving up.

  3. 03

    I Say a Little Prayer — Aretha Franklin

    Aretha Franklin

    She gets ready for work. Hair, makeup, pencil skirt. More put together. Feels a little more like herself.

  4. 04

    The Way You Look Tonight — Tony Bennett

    Tony Bennett

    She daydreams that she’s a bride at her first dance, with family and friends watching.

  5. 05

    If You Wanna Be Happy — Jimmy Soul

    Jimmy Soul

    The wedding crowd joins in. Half hit the dance floor, the other half start a ridiculous conga line.
