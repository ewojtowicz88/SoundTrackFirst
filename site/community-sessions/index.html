Workshops

14 / HOST

Host a session

One playlist. One rule set. One evening that cannot be repeated.

  1. 01

    Curate the score

    Build the Spotify playlist before anyone arrives, then send its URL to every participant. Fix the listening order and keep your interpretation to yourself.

  2. 02

    Set the duration

    Make each track a scene, beat, or page of time. Decide whether writers work alone or pass the story forward.

  3. 03

    Press play

    Let the music govern the room. No rewrites to chase later tracks; every turn has to earn its place.

  4. 04

    Hold the premiere

    Read the results, screen the fragments, or simply compare the films each person heard.

15 / PLAY

A playable brief

Scene one belongs to Beethoven. Scene two belongs to Interpol.

A shared playlist makes the format instantly legible: every writer receives the same sequence, then discovers a different film inside it.

Join a session
